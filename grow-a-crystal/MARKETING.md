## The honest constraint (read first)

Two things gate "market on all platforms" *today*:

1. **No gameplay media.** The game was never run (Studio access denied, Roblox Player not
   installed), so there is **no screenshot, GIF, or clip**. Every high-reach channel —
   r/roblox, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and even a good Roblox thumbnail/icon — needs media.
   Without it those channels are auto-removed or ignored.
2. **Not runtime-tested.** 166 unit tests + adversarial review, but no play-through. Driving
   players to a possibly-broken first run costs more reputation than it gains.

**Therefore the launch sequence is a loop, not a blast:**

> Recruit a handful of testers on dev subs (text posts, allowed with no media) → they
> validate the runtime **and** capture the first screenshots/clips → use that media to open
> the high-reach channels (r/roblox, TikTok, YT, a real thumbnail).

The tester-recruit posts below are safe to run now. Everything media-heavy is drafted and
**held** until a play-through + one screenshot exist.
