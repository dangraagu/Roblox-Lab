# Deep Vein - adversarial review

**Verdict: BLOCK**

Built and reviewed 2026-09-09/10. NOT published, and not to be published until the
findings below are closed. Every one of them was proved by running something, not by
reading - the proof is quoted with each.

## Findings (4)

### 1. MINED BLOCKS GROW BACK. Mine.reveal collects solid faces filtered ONLY by the call-local `visited` set — it never consults `opened`, although the air half of the same function does (`if not opened[k]` at the WALK loop). swing() clears cellParts[plr][cellKey] when a block breaks (:614). So the moment the player breaks ANY of the 26 neighbours of a cell they already mined, that cell is re-collected as a solid, openCell finds no Part for it (:373), and makeCellPart rebuilds it at FULL hit points with a fresh ClickDetector and a fresh ore payload. The generator is stateless by design, so kindAt still reports the mined cell as `rock` forever; `opened` is the only record that it was dug, and the solids path ignores it.

**Where:** `D:/Claude/Roblox/deep-vein/src/shared/Mine.luau:279 (Mine.reveal, the LOOK loop) together with D:/Claude/Roblox/deep-vein/src/server/Main.server.luau:373 (openCell) and :614 (swing)`

**What it costs a player:** This is not a cosmetic glitch, it is the core loop failing three ways at once. (1) UNBOUNDED CURRENCY DUPLICATION: the same ore cell can be farmed forever — mine it, mine any neighbour, mine it again. Cash, the rebirth economy, the pickaxe/backpack/lamp sinks and the global depth leaderboard are all denominated in a currency a player can print at will on their very first block. (2) THE DIG LOOP DOES NOT WORK: digging straight down is the most natural action in a mining game, and every cell you clear reseals behind you — after six layers the player is in a one-cell pocket with five full-strength blocks stacked overhead. (3) THE ELEVATOR TRAPS YOU: DESCEND aims at `deepestOpen`, which can be a cell you broke; once regrown, DESCEND CFrames the character into the centre of an anchored, CanCollide, full-size block. A player cannot reach any of this by accident-avoidance — the first two blocks they mine trigger it.

**Proof:** Proved end-to-end against the REAL server in the emulator, not by reading. (a) Pure-module sim mirroring openCell exactly: breaking 4:1:5 after 5:1:5 returned 5:1:5 in r.solids with opened[5:1:5]=true. (b) Real server via robloxemu (`py -3 wrap.py --game ../deep-vein` + luau): mined 5:1:5 to destruction, mined adjacent 4:1:5, then `blockAt("5:1:5")` returned `Rock_4242_5:1:5 size=6` with a live ClickDetector — the block came back at full cell size. (c) Duplication: copper cell 4:1:5 mined three times alternating with a neighbour, server-pushed `carried` went 1 -> 2 -> 3 from ONE cell. (d) Ceiling reseal: dug 5:1:5..5:6:5, then all of layers 1-5 reported `part present = true` at size=6 (5:2:5 came back as `Ore_`). (e) Elevator: mined 5:1:5 then 5:1:4; DESCEND landed in cell 5:1:5 with `occupied = true, Rock_4646_5:1:5 canCollide=true size=6`. ROOT CAUSE CONFIRMED BY FIX: adding `and not op[s.key]` to Main.server.luau:373 turned all four symptoms off in the same harness (A stays gone, RESURRECTED=false, carried stops at 1, resealed layers empty, DESCEND cell unoccupied). Note the whole existing gate is blind in BOTH directions: all 1627 unit assertions and all 87 headless assertions stay green with the bug present AND with the fix applied, so nothing enshrines the bug but nothing would catch a regression either. The principled fix is arguably in Mine.luau:279 (`and not opened[k]`), which would make it unit-testable in Mine.spec — I verified the server-side clause, not that one.

### 2. SURFACE teleports the player to a FIXED cell (centre, layer 0, z=2) at a fixed height, and the floor under that cell is ordinary breakable rock all the way down to bedrock. The README calls this out but describes it as a one-cell drop that is 'under a Roblox jump'. That understates it by an order of magnitude: nothing stops the player mining the entire column beneath the spawn point, and SURFACE keeps aiming at the same empty spot. The SELL pad sits at z=3, a different cell, so it does not catch the player either.

**Where:** `D:/Claude/Roblox/deep-vein/src/server/Main.server.luau:231 (spawnCFrame) and the SURFACE branch of the Elevator handler`

**What it costs a player:** Digging straight down where you are standing is the most natural possible first action, and the spawn cell is where you are standing. Every subsequent SURFACE press — the button that also sells your haul, so the button players press constantly — drops them straight back down the hole they just dug. The sale still lands, so no money is lost, but the button advertised as 'SURFACE + SELL' does not leave you on the surface. Currently INVISIBLE because finding 1 masks it: the regrow bug refills the column. Fixing finding 1 exposes this one, so they must be fixed together.

**Proof:** Measured in the emulator against the real server. With the source as shipped, digging 5:1:2 .. 5:12:2 then firing SURFACE reported `first solid cell beneath the spawn column: 1` — i.e. the regrow bug had refilled it, drop 0 studs. With the one-clause regrow fix applied and the bundle rebuilt, the identical run reported `first solid cell beneath the spawn column: 14 -> the player falls 78 studs (13 layers) the instant they arrive`. A separate pure-module probe confirmed the spawn cell's floor (5,1,2) is `kind=rock breakable=true` and that a straight drop from the spawn column reaches layer 24 = 144 studs.

### 3. Config.Mine.RevealBudget (1600) caps how many AIR cells one reveal may flood through, but NOTHING caps how many Parts the resulting solid list instantiates. openCell builds every revealed solid synchronously — Instance.new("Part"), a ClickDetector, four SetAttribute calls and a MouseClick connection each — inside the swing handler, with no task.wait() and no per-frame budget. buildShaft already has exactly the right pattern for this (it yields every 40 parts during the column restore at :477-486); the hot path simply does not use it.

**Where:** `D:/Claude/Roblox/deep-vein/src/server/Main.server.luau:356-383 (openCell), called from swing() at :619`

**What it costs a player:** The one swing that breaks into a large natural void — which the README sells as the payoff moment, 'a natural cave void that opens up all at once' — stalls the whole server, not just that player. Server-side Instance creation blocks the heartbeat, so every other miner in the server hitches too. The frame that should be the game's best moment is the frame it freezes.

**Proof:** Measured with a read-only probe over the real Mine/Ore/Config modules: found the largest connected void per seed, then ran Mine.reveal from inside it with a fresh `opened` table (exactly what openCell hands to makeCellPart) and counted #rev.solids. Rebirth 0: 144-cell void -> 334 Parts in one frame. Rebirth 7: 349 -> 807. Rebirth 16: 801 -> 1989. Rebirth 24: 1137 -> 3031 Parts, 3031 ClickDetectors, ~12k attribute writes and 3031 signal connections in a single frame, `truncated = false` in every case (so the reveal budget never even engages).

### 4. Obsidian's MinLayer (26) is deeper than the rebirth-0 depth wall (24), so Ore.weight returns 0 for it at every reachable layer and the tier cannot spawn at all before the first rebirth. README bullet 3 lists it as an ore you find deeper ('gold, diamond and obsidian only deeper'), alongside two tiers that genuinely are reachable. The builder's own hand-off notes claim the paste-ready description 'under-sells' by omitting obsidian; the truth is the reverse for a pre-rebirth player. Same census also shows only 6 diamond cells exist in the ENTIRE rebirth-0 shaft.

**Where:** `D:/Claude/Roblox/deep-vein/src/shared/Config.luau — Config.Ores.MinLayer[5] = 26 vs Config.Mine.BaseMaxLayer = 24`

**What it costs a player:** A player reads the README or store description, hunts the deepest layers of the base game for a purple ore that is not there, and concludes the drop rate is broken or that they are unlucky. It is neither — it is unreachable. And with 6 diamond cells in ~842 rock cells, the tier just above it is close to invisible too without near-total excavation.

**Proof:** Read-only census over the real Mine/Ore/Config modules, walking every cell of the sealed box per rebirth: `rebirth 0: rock=842 ... ores: Stone=642 Copper=106 Iron=64 Gold=24 Diamond=6 Obsidian=0`, and the explicit gate table printed `Obsidian MinLayer=26 reachable at rebirth 0 (maxLayer 24)? false`. Obsidian first appears at rebirth 1 (4 cells).

## What the review could NOT break (12)

- PROCESS INCIDENT — NOT A GAME DEFECT, BUT READ THIS. D:/Claude/Roblox has several sibling agents writing concurrently (fork-tower, vault-runners, nightwatch-manor and robloxemu all had mtimes inside my review window). My scratchpad backup directory collided with another reviewer's: both of us used the same session scratchpad path and the same `backup/` folder name, so `cp -r <game>/src $SP/backup/src` MERGED the two games. My restore then wrote nightwatch-manor's Main.server.luau, Config.luau, Hud.client.luau and Fx.luau plus five stray modules (Manor, Night, Rng, Upgrades, Watcher) into deep-vein/src. I caught it because I hash every file before touching anything and the post-restore hash did not match. RECOVERY: deep-vein is untracked in git (`?? deep-vein/`) so git could not help; I reconstructed all ten sources byte-exactly from robloxemu/build/deep-vein.luau, which stores every source verbatim in a long string and had been built from the deep-vein tree minutes earlier, reverted my one-clause probe fix, and deleted the five strays. VERIFIED: all 18 baseline sha256 hashes match exactly, byte counts match the original directory listing, 1627 unit assertions and 87 headless assertions are green, and the bundle has been rebuilt from the restored (unfixed) tree. nightwatch-manor was checked read-only and is INTACT (its files won the merge; it contains no deep-vein modules). Nothing was committed, pushed or published; git HEAD is unchanged at b92c316. Recommendation for future parallel reviews: per-agent unique backup paths, and hash-verify restores rather than trusting them.
- TEST COUNTS REPRODUCE EXACTLY. Ore 1260/0, Mine 86/0, Economy 122/0, Prestige 89/0, Responsive 70/0 = 1627 assertions, 0 failed, run with the real luau.exe. Headless: `py -3 wrap.py --game ../deep-vein --out build/deep-vein.luau` then `luau check_deepvein.luau` -> `deep vein: 87 passed, 0 failed`, zero server warnings. Every number the builder reported is honest. (Note the check file is check_deepvein.luau, not check_deep-vein.luau as the brief spelled it.)
- NO UNPARENTED INSTANCE. I enumerated all 61 Instance.new sites across the four engine-aware files and accounted for every one mechanically. The single flag my script raised — Main.server.luau:272 `local part = Instance.new("Part")` — is a false positive from a 40-line search window; `part.Parent = folder` lands at :335 after the ClickDetector is attached, which is deliberate and commented. All Fx/FxClient/Hud sites parent within a few lines. The mutation sweep confirms the gate is real: deleting `part.Parent = folder` crashes the headless check immediately.
- NO RELATIVE SIBLING REQUIRES IN SHIPPED CODE. Every require in src/ is `require(ReplicatedStorage:WaitForChild("X"))` — 4 in Hud.client, 6 in Main.server, zero in src/shared. The pure modules genuinely take cfg/Ore/Economy as arguments. The `require("../src/shared/X")` forms exist only in tests/, which never ship to Roblox.
- GetDataStore IS WRAPPED. Both stores go through a `tryStore` helper that pcalls and degrades to nil with a warning (Main.server.luau:62-74), covering GetDataStore and GetOrderedDataStore. Every subsequent call site is nil-guarded, and the leaderboard SetAsync/GetSortedAsync are separately pcall'd. An unpublished place will run the game with saving disabled rather than dying at load.
- MUTATION SWEEP: 8/8 REAL DEFECTS KILLED, 2/2 CONTROLS SURVIVED. I mutated the SOURCE (not any in-memory model) and ran the full gate — all five specs plus a rebuilt bundle plus the headless check — for each. RED as required: delete `part.Parent = folder`; `if left > 0` -> `>= 0`; drop the `clicker ~= plr` owner guard; Ore.weight drops the MinLayer gate; addOre ignores capacity; kindAt drops the bedrock floor; delete the swing cooldown; Prestige.apply also wipes bestLayer. GREEN as required (proving the gate discriminates rather than just alarms): deleting `part.CastShadow = false`, and AutosaveSeconds 20 -> 25. Baseline green before, green after, and every source file restored to its baseline hash.
- luau-analyze IS CLEAN on all ten sources with only the genuine Roblox 'Unknown global'/'Unknown type' noise filtered — no other diagnostics on any file.
- THE REVEAL BUDGET HOLDS FOR EVERY REACHABLE SEED, which the builder listed as an untested unknown. I measured the largest connected void for all 26 rebirth seeds (0..MaxRebirths=25), not just seed 0. Worst case is 1137 cells at rebirth 24 against a budget of 1600, and `truncated` was false in every reveal I ran. No seed can produce the 'hole with nothing behind it' failure. (Headroom at the worst seed is only 1.4x, and that same void is the 3031-Part frame spike in finding 3.)
- THE CAVE IS GENUINELY DETERMINISTIC AND ORDER-INDEPENDENT. I could not make cell contents depend on dig order: the stateless mixer means kindAt/cell are pure functions of (seed,x,y,z), which my own out-of-order probes confirmed. The in-suite controls are real too — Mine.spec's shadow config with every ore renamed, recoloured and repriced moves 0 cells, and each control block ends with a change the same comparison DOES notice, so it cannot pass vacuously.
- THE SEALED BOX HOLDS. Bedrock ring at x/z = 1 and GridW, bedrock floor one layer past the wall, and inWorld clamps y to [0, maxLayer+1]. The layer-0 rim really is built 3 cells (18 studs) tall against a ~7.2-stud Roblox jump, verified in the workspace by the headless check. I could not find a way to leave the shaft laterally or through the floor — the only way out of the world is the open top, which is what finding 2 is about.
- THE onPlayerAdded YIELD RACE IS CORRECTLY GUARDED. I traced the interleaving by hand: during loadProfile's UpdateAsync yield, profiles[plr] is still nil so a concurrent onPlayerRemoving frees nothing and errors nothing, and the `if plr.Parent == nil then profiles[plr] = nil; return end` check then aborts the shaft build. No leaked shaft index, no orphan folder. As the builder says, the emulator's synchronous DataStore cannot drive this — I verified it by reading, not by running, so it remains unproven by execution.
- STILL UNVERIFIED BY ANYONE, INCLUDING ME: nothing renders, so no material, colour, lighting or camera choice is asserted anywhere; MaxActivationDistance/click reach is never exercised because the harness fires ClickDetectors directly; and no real character controller has ever touched this world, so falling, collision and the 'embedded in a block' consequence in finding 1 are proven only as CFrame/geometry facts, not as observed humanoid behaviour. A Studio session remains mandatory before publish.
