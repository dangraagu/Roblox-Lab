## What players want right now (convergent across all 6 scouts)

1. **"Steal-and-collect" is still the single most repeated viral template** (Steal a
   Brainrot → Steal An Egg → Steal a Chicken), with Steal An Egg sitting at #1 on all of
   Roblox (1.4–2.2M concurrent, 275M+ visits) and Steal a Chicken up +1,407.1% in 7-day
   growth. But every scout also flags fatigue and backlash: Roblox's own earnings call
   blames part of a $70B value drop on brainrot-genre burnout, TikTok creators are calling
   Steal a Brainrot "dying," and clone lawsuits are already flying. **Verdict: the mechanic
   is proven, the brainrot skin is exhausted — steal loops need an original theme and a
   scoped-down (NPC-guard, not raid-PvP) build to be worth a solo dev's time.**
2. **Obby/platformer remains the most solo-accessible genre by a wide margin** — Tower of
   Hell's baseline (165K avg CCU, 24.3B visits) proves the floor traffic, "Would You Rather"
   choice-towers are up +899.7% in 7-day growth as a whole new sub-genre breaking out at
   once, and the steal/obby hybrid Jump for Animals is up +407.9% at 219K concurrent. This
   genre reuses our exact procedural-maze + DataStore stack almost directly.
3. **Tycoon/idle-simulator mechanics are the platform's most mechanically simple, most
   solo-buildable archetypes** — prestige loops, automation (droppers/conveyors/upgraders),
   and digging/mining are all well-documented, low-risk builds. Two independent scouts flag
   **horror-tycoon hybrids** specifically as an emerging, low-competition niche just as the
   Sept–Oct Halloween window delivers a documented 3x CCU multiplier for horror.
4. **Procedural depth is underused inside proven-demand genres** — the digging/mining
   simulator space (830M+ combined visits) is almost entirely flat, hand-built maps with a
   clicker bolted on; genuine procedural cave generation is a structural differentiator we
   already have the tech for. Same story in obby: nothing trending combines the rage-obby
   loop with a pet/collector meta, despite the adjacent hybrid already proving out.
5. **Choice-branching towers ("Would You Rather") are a fast-breaking, currently
   under-served sub-genre** — near-maximal buildability (branching path + simple choice UI,
   zero PvP/AI/audio), but trivially copyable once noticed, so the low-competition window is
   likely short.

Cross-cutting edge: **procedural generation + DataStore persistence is this dev's actual
moat** — every top opportunity below either fills a gap the market hasn't combined yet
(obby × collector, mining × real caves) or captures a seasonal window (horror × Halloween)
using tech already shipped. "`<game>` codes" / prestige / rebirth keywords remain a strong,
underused SEO lever across all of these.

**Avoid solo:** raw 1:1 brainrot/steal clones (saturated + litigated + fatigue-flagged),
full raid-PvP base defense, competitive combat/anime fighters (netcode + content treadmill),
AAA racing, full multiplayer co-op horror requiring pathfinding AI.

## Concept briefs (top 4)

### 1. Vault Runners: Obby & Collect!  ·  score 8.1

- **Hook:** Every run, the vault regenerates and starts collapsing the second you step in —
  grab the gems, beat the seal, and bank your loot to hatch vault-keeper pets before the
  next floor gets meaner.
- **Core loop:** Drop into a freshly procedurally-generated vault floor → climb/obby through
  it grabbing gems while a visible collapse (rising lava, sealing doors, crumbling
  platforms) chases you on a timer → escape (or die trying) — gems only bank if you get out
  before the seal → spend banked gems at the hub to hatch/upgrade Vault-Keeper pets and buy
  run-boosts → unlock the next harder floor tier and repeat, with DataStore saving gems,
  pets, and floor-unlock progress between sessions.
- **Why it trends:** Sits exactly in the gap two scouts flagged: obby has a massive proven
  baseline (Tower of Hell: 165K avg CCU, 24.3B visits) but nothing trending combines that
  rage-obby loop with a pet/collector meta — the adjacent hybrid (Jump for Animals:
  jump-tower + egg-stealing) is already up +407.9% growth at 219K concurrent, proving
  players want exactly this cross-genre mashup.
- **Solo build scope:** Reuse the existing 500-level procedural maze/obby generator,
  re-skinned as vertical "vault floors," with 3 difficulty tiers (Bronze/Silver/Gold vault)
  unlocked by total gems banked. Collapse mechanic = a single scripted rising kill-plane/
  closing-door system on a countdown timer — no new physics, just a timed CFrame/size tween.
  One server-side gem-spawn pass per generated floor plus a banked-vs-lost check at the exit
  trigger. DataStore schema: playerGems, ownedPets, unlockedTier — same pattern as the
  existing checkpoint/skip-save system. Hub hatch-pet stall: flat table of 6-10 pets gated by
  gem cost, each just a follow-script + small stat multiplier — no breeding/trading/eggs
  complexity for v1. Ship with 1 pet rarity tier and 3 vault tiers, expand post-launch.
- **Genre tag:** Obby (primary) / Collector-Adventure (subgenre) — "Procedural Rage-Obby +
  Pet Collector" hybrid tag.
- **Thumbnail idea:** A player mid-air leaping between two crumbling stone platforms, vault
  walls visibly sealing/glowing red behind them, a cluster of oversized glittering gems in
  the foreground, and a small winged Vault-Keeper pet flying alongside — bold "VAULT
  RUNNERS" text with a cracked-vault-door frame around the whole image.

### 2. Nightwatch Manor: Haunted Escape Tycoon  ·  score 7.3

- **Hook:** Survive a new haunted manor every night, loot its relics, then spend them
  building a trap-filled safehouse strong enough to keep the dark out for good.
- **Core loop:** NIGHT PHASE — drop into a procedurally-laid-out manor (modular room-piece
  generator), explore, solve light environmental puzzles, dodge a scripted chase-trigger
  horror (patrol/line-of-sight/jumpscare triggers, no AI/netcode needed), grab relics + cash,
  extract before the manor "wakes up" fully. DAY PHASE — back at the persistent safehouse
  hub, spend relics/cash (DataStore-saved) on traps, turrets, alarms, lighting, and
  structural upgrades that make the next night's manor survivable — a classic tycoon loop
  bolted onto the horror run, with difficulty and manor size scaling with hub level.
- **Why it trends:** Two independent scouts flag horror-tycoon hybrids as an emerging
  sub-genre with almost no competition yet. Horror is Roblox's #1 genre by content volume
  (185K YouTube videos/month) with top-50 horror titles running 47% above-average CCU, and
  layering tycoon/progression on top answers the documented ceiling on solo-only horror.
  September–October carries a proven 3x CCU seasonal multiplier for horror, and today
  (Sept 9) is the ideal launch runway to be live and indexed before the Halloween spike.
- **Solo build scope:** A modular room-kit + seeded procedural layout script (reused every
  night — the biggest differentiation-to-effort payoff). One scripted "Nightwatcher" enemy
  using waypoint patrol + line-of-sight/proximity triggers for chases and jumpscares — zero
  pathfinding AI or netcode. A relic/loot pickup system feeding a DataStore-backed currency.
  A single persistent safehouse hub (tycoon-style placeable traps/defenses, saved via
  DataStore) gating access to harder/larger manor seeds. Basic extraction win-condition +
  death/reset flow. Skip for v1: multiplayer co-op sync, voice/proximity chat, cosmetics
  shop, leaderboard events.
- **Genre tag:** Horror (primary) with Tycoon as secondary/cross-genre tag — list as Horror
  on Roblox, and use "Tycoon," "Adventure," and "Survival" as supporting keywords since
  Roblox's genre picker has no native combined "Horror-Tycoon" category.
- **Thumbnail idea:** Split-composition: left half a shadowy manor hallway with a pale,
  twisted figure lunging into frame lit by a flashlight beam (horror hook, high contrast),
  right half a warm, lit-up safehouse room bristling with turrets/traps and a glowing relic
  on a pedestal — bold white outlined text "SURVIVE. LOOT. BUILD." across the middle seam.

### 3. Deep Vein: Mining Simulator  ·  score 7.05

- **Hook:** Every dive drills through a truly procedural cave — no map is ever the same, no
  matter how deep you go.
- **Core loop:** Drop into the mine shaft → procedurally generated cave terrain unfolds
  beneath you → dig through rock/rubble to expose ore veins (common copper/iron up top, rare
  gold/diamond/obsidian deeper) → mine veins with your pickaxe (swing speed/power scales with
  tool tier) → haul ore back to the surface elevator or auto-sell on pickup → spend cash on
  pickaxe/drill upgrades, backpack capacity, and light radius → push deeper into harder,
  richer biomes → hit a depth/cash wall → Prestige/Rebirth: wipe cash + depth progress for a
  permanent multiplier → repeat the dig with a stronger loadout and newly regenerated caves.
- **Why it trends:** The parent digging/mining + simulator space pulls 830M+ combined
  visits, but almost every existing entry is a flat, hand-built map with a clicker loop
  bolted on — no real terrain variety to keep long-session players engaged. Real procedural
  cave generation is a structural differentiator inside a proven-demand genre, not just a
  skin change, and borrows the exact gather → sell → upgrade → prestige skeleton behind
  current top-chart hits like Grow a Garden and Pet Simulator 99.
- **Solo build scope:** Chunk-based procedural cave generator (noise-driven voxel/part
  carving) that streams terrain downward as the player digs — reused directly from prior
  procedural work. 4-5 ore tiers spawned via rarity weighting keyed to depth. Single
  tool-swing mining interaction: hit rock/ore, deal damage, destroy/collect — no combat, no
  PvP, no server-authoritative contention since each player mines their own generated shaft.
  Simple shop UI: pickaxe tiers, drill/light-radius upgrades, backpack capacity, all
  cash-gated. Rebirth/Prestige button resets cash+depth, applies a permanent multiplier, and
  regenerates a fresh (harder) cave seed. DataStore-backed save: cash, depth, tool tier,
  rebirth count, ore inventory. One global OrderedDataStore leaderboard (deepest dig or total
  cash). No trading, no PvP, no real-time multiplayer interaction required.
- **Genre tag:** Simulator > Mining/Digging Simulator.
- **Thumbnail idea:** A player avatar mid-swing with a glowing pickaxe, standing at the
  jagged edge of a dark, irregular (clearly non-flat) cave shaft that plunges downward —
  glowing blue/gold ore veins visible embedded in the rock walls below, dramatic rim
  lighting, bold chunky text overlay reading "DIG DEEPER" in the corner.

### 4. Fork Tower: Would You Rather Obby!  ·  score 6.95

- **Hook:** Every door is a would-you-rather — and every answer rebuilds your character,
  your loadout, and the tower itself beneath your feet.
- **Core loop:** Spawn in the lobby/hub and approach a Choice Door showing two
  would-you-rather prompts (e.g. "Fire Boots" vs "Ice Grapple") → pick one — the game
  instantly applies a stat/skin/ability modifier via attributes and teleports you into a
  procedurally-selected obby section tagged for that choice → climb that section
  (checkpoints + killbricks as normal), then hit the next Choice Door and pick again — each
  pick narrows/reshapes the next section's theme, hazards, and cosmetics → after ~8-12
  branching floors, reach the summit and hit the "Build Reveal" — a stat/skin/trait card
  generated purely from your choice history, saved to DataStore, screenshot-and-share ready
  → Rebirth to reroll the choice tree/seed and chase a rarer "build," driving replay and
  sharing loops.
- **Why it trends:** "Would You Rather: Anime Tower" is up +899.7% in 7-day growth with a
  near-identical variant also charting simultaneously — a rare signal of a whole sub-genre
  breaking out at once, not a one-off fluke. Choice-towers are explicitly flagged as
  underserved: high momentum, very few high-quality entrants. Players are actively typing
  "would you rather tower" and "choice obby" into Roblox search right now, before the format
  gets saturated by copycats.
- **Solo build scope:** A single-server hub with modular obby kit pieces (platforms,
  killbricks, checkpoints) already built, retagged/recolored per choice-branch theme
  (fire/ice/speed/troll etc.) rather than built from scratch. A lightweight Choice Door: a
  ProximityPrompt or touch trigger opening a simple two-button ScreenGui with the
  would-you-rather text — no dialogue system required. A small server-side table mapping
  (floor, choiceID) → section variant + stat modifier, driving procedural section selection
  by index/weighted-random reusing existing procedural-gen logic. Player choice history +
  earned build stored via DataStore for persistence and the reveal screen. A Build Reveal
  screen that renders a title/stat card client-side (pure UI, no external image gen).
  Rebirth = reset DataStore choice log + reroll seed, reusing the existing rebirth/reset
  pattern. Target: ~1-2 weeks to a shippable MVP with 8-12 branch floors and 2 choices each.
- **Genre tag:** Obby — "Would You Rather" / Choice-Branching Tower subgenre.
- **Thumbnail idea:** Split-screen "before you choose" composition: a glowing door cracked
  down the middle, left side bathed in red/fire light with a fire-themed character variant,
  right side in cyan/ice light with an ice-themed variant, tower silhouette climbing into the
  sky behind both. Bold white/yellow outlined text across the top reading "WOULD YOU
  RATHER?" with a small "?!" burst icon — high contrast, mirrors the visual language of the
  charting "Would You Rather" titles.
