## What players want right now (convergent across all 6 scouts)

1. **"Steal-a-X" + idle-income is the dominant demand vector** — Steal An Egg went 0→#1
   (~1.87M concurrent) in a month; Steal a Brainrot is the only Roblox game ever past 25M
   CCU (60B+ visits). **But** it's the most saturated *and* most legally hazardous space
   (4+ clone lawsuits) with real moderation-removal risk (Roblox pulled Steal An Egg hours
   after #1 in a gen-AI "brainrot slop" crackdown). **Verdict: borrow the loop, drop the
   brainrot skin, ship original IP.**
2. **The winning formula is simple and repeatable:** one instantly-legible idea + constant
   visible progression (a number that rises every few seconds) + thumbnail/clip legibility +
   light social stakes. Complexity does *not* drive virality (+1 Speed Keyboard Escape hit
   2.55–3B visits at ~98% approval on a one-sentence idea, 6.35M concurrent peak).
3. **Incremental "+1 per step" escape/obby = the top solo sweet spot** — reuses our exact
   stack (procedural-gen + DataStore + single-server, simple UI), zero combat/netcode, MVP
   in days-to-weeks.
4. **Cozy / anti-grind is a real rising counter-trend** (Grow a Garden ~1M+ concurrent,
   fastest ever to 1B visits; Fisch/Fish It! climbing as players fatigue on frantic
   brainrot) — solo-buildable, less clone-saturated, lower moderation risk.
5. **Co-op survival horror is surging and Roblox-endorsed** (99 Nights ~14.2M peak; Animal
   Hospital ~700K; official Fall preview) but is the most production-heavy top genre — the
   solo-approachable slice is the **Doors-style procedural roguelite** (single/lean co-op),
   which maps ~1:1 onto our procedural-gen + DataStore skills.

Cross-cutting edge: **procedural + persistent-save depth is rare** in the top idle/obby/horror
titles (most are hand-built and shallow) — our genuine differentiator for near-infinite runs
and long-tail retention. "`<game>` codes" is a massive, underused SEO lever. Well-run solo
obby/idle devs report ~$20K–27K/month on fair monetization (cosmetics, skip/convenience passes).

**Avoid solo:** 1:1 brainrot/steal clones (saturated + litigated + removal risk), competitive
FPS, anime fighters (combat netcode + content treadmill), AAA racing, full-scale co-op horror.

## Concept briefs (top 4)

### 1. +1 Jump Every Step 🔼 Sky Temple Obby  ·  score 8.6

- **Hook:** Every tile you touch makes your jump higher — leap over the tall ruins that trap
  everyone else and climb an endless sky temple to the clouds.
- **Core loop:** Walk forward → each tile grants +1 Jump Power (HUD number ticks up) → bigger
  jump clears the next taller ruin tier → endless procedural tower never repeats → REBIRTH to
  bank a permanent jump multiplier and rocket past your old wall → codes + gamepasses
  (Auto-Walk, 2× Jump, Sky Trails). Forward progress *is* number-go-up (zero grind friction).
- **Why it trends:** Rides the proven "+1 per step" formula (see +1 Speed Keyboard Escape) but
  swaps the stat (jump, not the flooded speed thumbnails) and theme (original sky temple), so
  it reads fresh in search and the algorithm; procedural variety gives the depth one-note
  rivals lack.
- **Solo build scope:** MVP in days-to-weeks reusing our exact stack. Endless tower generator
  (spawn ahead / despawn behind, template pool) + server-authoritative JumpPower (+1 per new
  tile, per-tile debounce) → Humanoid.JumpHeight + HUD; DataStore for jump/tier/rebirth/
  multiplier/gamepasses (session-lock to prevent dupes); rebirth button; minimal UI + Ordered
  DataStore top-10. Cut pets/trading/quests for v1. Art = Toolbox temple kit recolored per tier.
- **Genre tag:** Obby & Platformer → Tower Obby (secondary descriptors: Simulator / Incremental).
- **Thumbnail:** split shot — left, tiny hop slamming a huge ruin wall (red ✗, sad "+1"); right,
  same avatar mid-epic-leap soaring over with a trail + giant green "+1 JUMP!" and counter
  "JUMP: 9,999"; banner "+1 JUMP EACH STEP!". High saturation, tiny-player-vs-towering-ruins.

  while you're offline — then fortify your observatory before raiders steal it back.
- **Core loop:** Sneak onto a rival island → steal a rarity-gated Lumin before the alarm timer
  → dock it in your observatory where it mints ⭐ Starlight passively (offline earnings) → spend
  on pods/gear/locks → fortify vs counter-raids → escalating rarity + prestige. Original
  bioluminescent IP (no memes/brands).
- **Why it trends:** The single dominant demand vector; original IP dodges the 4+ clone lawsuits
  *and* the AI-slop moderation takedowns that hit the leaders. Idle offline-income layer hooks
  the return/notification retention loop.
- **Solo build scope:** Ship a **PvE-leaning MVP that defers real-time PvP raiding** (the one
  part needing hard anti-abuse). 8–12 procedural islands; server-validated hold-to-grab + escape

  Lumins across 5 tiers; shop + prestige + codes; counter-raids v1 = **async "ghost raid"** on a
  snapshot (locks vs tools, server-resolved) — steal-back fantasy, zero real-time netcode.
  ~2–3 weeks to playable, ~5–6 to polished.
- **Genre tag:** Simulation → Idle / Tycoon (Steal & Collect subgenre).
- **Thumbnail:** avatar mid-leap yanking a radiant Lumin from its pod (energy trail), Starlight

### 3. Grow a Mushroom Cave 🍄🧬  ·  score 7.85

- **Hook:** Grow a glowing underground mushroom cave that keeps growing while you're offline —
  then cross-breed rare mutant strains no one else on Roblox has.
- **Core loop:** Plant spore → grows offline (timestamp math on rejoin) → harvest + sell → buy
  rarer strains/plots/glow → **cross-breed two strains** to roll a weighted mutation
  (color/glow/size/rarity/value) → chase 1-in-X galaxy/rainbow rolls → collect in a Strain-Dex,
  trade, replant. Collect-and-gamble dopamine on a zero-stress cozy idle base.
- **Why it trends:** Offline-growth cozy farming is the biggest solo-shaped pattern (Grow a
  Garden proof); the crossbreed-genetics hook adds share-worthy mutation moments (screenshots,
  trade-bait, "what did you roll?") that clones lack.
- **Solo build scope:** Per-player cavern (instance in a small shared server), procedural plot
  grid; offline growth via timestamp (no server tick); DataStore for coins/spores/plots/dex;
  8–12 base strains + weighted mutation table with rare tiers; emissive glow + PointLights;
  shop + Strain-Dex UI + codes. Cut real trading (ship gift/showcase first). Build is
  UI + data + RNG; ongoing cost = content cadence.
- **Genre tag:** Simulation → Idle / Incremental Simulator (cozy farming & collection).
- **Thumbnail:** dark bioluminescent cave, one GIANT rainbow-galaxy mutant shroom centered,
  shocked avatar, yellow arrow, "MUTATED?!" / "1 IN 1,000,000". High-contrast neon-on-dark.

### 4. FATHOM 🌊 Deep Sea Escape Horror  ·  score 7.3

- **Hook:** Escape a flooding deep-sea station that rebuilds itself every run — no two dives are
  ever the same, and the abyss is always hungry.
- **Core loop:** Descend → move door-to-door through freshly generated flooding rooms, managing
  oxygen/flashlight/sonar → evade creatures, grab salvage, reach the hatch (or die and lose the
  run's loot) → bank salvage in the surface hub for permanent gear → re-dive deeper into a new
  layout. Solo-first, optional duo.
- **Why it trends:** Horror is surging and Roblox-endorsed, but single-player/roguelite horror
  is under-supplied. Rides the understood Doors escape format, adds procedural layouts +
  persistent gear (rare depth), and fresh-every-run maps are perfect fuel for the "I Survived /
  how deep did you get?" clip format.
- **Solo build scope:** Single-server solo-first (duo later) to dodge the co-op netcode penalty;
  ~15–20 modular room prefabs + seeded stitcher (door-to-door path); DataStore for salvage +
  gear unlocks (run loot lost on death, banked salvage kept); ONE chaser creature (simple state
  machine) + one "don't-look" hazard; oxygen/flashlight/sonar resource loop; hub shop + "deepest
  descent" leaderboard. Remaining real cost = **audio/atmosphere** (budget a small sound pack).
- **Genre tag:** Survival → Survival Horror (roguelite Escape Room); reinforce with Horror /
  Escape / Roguelite descriptors.
- **Thumbnail:** lone diver, flashlight beam through green-black water; huge anglerfish silhouette
  behind a rusted airlock; near-empty red oxygen bar, cracked helmet, "HOW DEEP?". Dark frame,
  one bright light source.
