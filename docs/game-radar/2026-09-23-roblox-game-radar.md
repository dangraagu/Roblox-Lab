## What players want right now (convergent across all 6 scouts)

Six scouts converge on a small set of clear signals for a solo proc-gen/DataStore/single-server
developer:

1. **Horror is the standout cross-platform opportunity.** DevForum's genre report shows horror
   CCU jumping 46.1K→77.7K (**+68% WoW**); Roblox's own Fall Games Preview is investing in an
   AAA-pedigree horror title (ex-Marvel Studios visual designer); TikTok flags horror as its
   highest-velocity, commentary-free reaction-clip genre; and the buildability scout
   independently rates procedural co-op horror as the single best demand+speed match for this
   exact developer, because room/corridor generation is a direct transplant of maze-generation
   code. Most current horror titles reset almost entirely per round — persistent DataStore
   progression is the underexploited hook.
2. **Obby is quietly reviving via horror hybridization** — charting "keyboard escape" obby
   (266K+ concurrent) and 2026 roundup callouts for horror-parkour crossovers like "Escape the
   Carnival of Terror." This is a near-zero-cost extension of our existing 500-level engine:
   reskin + a chase-trigger layer, not a rebuild.
3. **Steal-and-collect/idle-simulator loops remain the single largest raw-demand category**
   (1.4M CCU cluster, Steal An Egg's #1 trending debut, Grow a Garden's records) but show clear
   fatigue and brainrot-skin saturation/litigation risk (**-78.1% WoW** for the leading clone).
   Verdict: borrow the mechanic under an original theme rather than compete head-on on the
   litigated brainrot skin.
4. **Town/City tycoon is the single highest quantitative opportunity score** (71.6/100) in this
   week's genre-trend report — **+65% WoW growth against only 34% saturation** — distinct from
   the meme-driven categories, with almost no direct competitor coverage elsewhere.
5. **Round-based/procedural survival and "daily-seed" retention mechanics are named as
   underexploited hooks** that reward exactly the proc-gen + DataStore skillset already in
   hand — no breakout daily-seed title exists yet ("Wordle for dungeon crawls" is wide open).

**Avoid solo:** anime fighting/combat (PvP netcode, 40-60+ animations, explicit burnout
warnings across scouts) and Roleplay/Avatar-sim (asset-pipeline and live-ops heavy despite being
the single largest raw demand category).

## Concept briefs (top 4)

### 1. Signal Lost: Derelict Station  ·  score 8.5

- **Hook:** You're the last signal on a dying space station — outrun a hunting Entity through
  corridors that reshuffle every run, and keep what you earn.
- **Core loop:** Spawn solo (or with friends) at the airlock of a procedurally-generated derelict
  station. Explore reshuffling corridors and rooms to find keycards/signal-fragments while
  managing noise, stamina, and hiding spots as a stalking entity actively hunts by sound/light.
  Reach the extraction point to "win" the run and bank Data — spend it in the hub on permanent
  perks (extra stamina, better flashlight battery, quieter footsteps) and cosmetics via
  DataStore, so every run — win or die — makes the next attempt stronger. Die, and you keep your
  banked unlocks but lose the run's loot, creating a roguelite risk/reward loop layered on top of
  the horror chase.
- **Why it trends:** Horror is the single strongest convergent signal across every source in the
  dataset right now: DevForum's genre-trend report shows horror CCU jumping 46.1K→77.7K (+68%
  WoW), the fastest-moving genre on the platform. Roblox itself is doubling down with its Fall
  Games Preview funding an AAA-pedigree horror title (ex-Marvel Studios visual designer),
  signaling platform-level promotion for the genre. TikTok independently flags horror as its
  highest-velocity, commentary-free reaction-clip genre — meaning jump-scare and chase moments
  from this game are inherently clippable, shareable content that drives organic discovery
  without needing influencer scripts. Meanwhile the crowded DOORS/Apeirophobia/Pressure field all
  resets progress every round; persistent DataStore-banked perks/cosmetics is the specific
  underexploited hook that lets this game differentiate on retention rather than needing new
  content weekly.
- **Solo build scope:** MVP scoped for 1-3 weeks solo, reusing existing strengths: (1) Procedural
  generation — repurpose maze/procedural-gen code into a room-and-corridor tile system for the
  station (10-15 room modules, randomized connections/seed per run); (2) One entity AI — single
  stalking enemy using Roblox pathfinding with basic states (patrol/investigate/hunt) reacting to
  player noise and flashlight use, no complex behavior tree needed; (3) One map theme — derelict
  space station only (skip multiple biomes for v1); (4) DataStore perk/cosmetic bank — a small
  fixed catalog (5-8 perks, 5-8 cosmetics) purchased with a single "Data" currency earned per
  successful extraction, persisted via DataStoreService; (5) Single-server, small-lobby (2-6
  players) with no matchmaking/cross-server tech required; (6) Minimal UI — objective tracker,
  stamina/noise meter, hub shop screen. Cut for v1: leaderboards, seasonal events, multiple
  entities, voice chat, mobile-specific control polish.
- **Genre tag:** Horror → Survival / Roguelite (Escape-Horror Corridor Crawler).
- **Thumbnail:** a dark, flickering derelict-station corridor lit only by a handheld flashlight
  beam and red emergency strip-lights. In the foreground, a player character (back turned,
  running toward camera) casts a long shadow; deep in the corridor behind them, barely visible in
  the dark, two glowing red/white eyes and a humanoid silhouette of the Entity emerge from the
  shadows. Bold white/red distressed text overlay: "RUN." with a small "SIGNAL LOST" logo badge
  in a corner — high contrast, mobile-thumbnail readable, evokes DOORS-style dread but distinct
  with a sci-fi/space-station color palette (cool blues/grays + alarm red) instead of DOORS' warm
  hotel tones.

### 2. Asylum Escape: Horror Obby (500 Levels)  ·  score 8.3

- **Hook:** A 500-level keyboard-escape obby where a chaser entity is hunting you through a
  collapsing, procedurally-lit asylum — miss a jump and it catches you.
- **Core loop:** Player spawns at their last DataStore checkpoint and parkours forward through
  procedurally-lit asylum corridors (reskinned from the existing 500-level maze engine). A
  chaser entity trails behind on a timer/proximity trigger — falling, missing a jump, or lingering
  too long lets it close the gap; getting caught sends the player back to their last checkpoint
  (not to zero), preserving the "keyboard escape, +1 speed" pacing players already chase in top
  obby games. Scripted jumpscares (flickering lights, door slams, silhouette flashes) fire at set
  level milestones to build dread without needing new mechanics. Reaching new checkpoints saves
  progress via DataStore, a leaderboard tracks deepest level reached, and speed/mobility boosts
  (the "+1 Speed" hook) let skilled players outrun the chaser and climb the board.
- **Why it trends:** Two independent signals point at the same gap: the YouTube scout's 2026 obby
  roundup singles out "Escape the Carnival of Terror" specifically for fusing horror atmosphere
  with parkour, framing it as proof the obby genre is "reviving specifically via horror
  crossovers" — and that same research explicitly names a dev with a working 500-level maze/obby
  engine as the ideal candidate to add horror lighting, chase mechanics, and jump-scares as a
  reskin layer. Meanwhile TikTok data shows the "+1 Speed Keyboard Escape" obby format still
  charts top-5 concurrent with 266K+ players, meaning casual obby demand is alive and just needs a
  fresh hook — and nobody has strongly merged that charting keyboard-escape format with a
  horror/chase hook yet. That combination of proven demand and zero direct competition in the
  merged niche is what gives this a 10/10 buildability, since it's a reskin plus a chase-trigger
  layer over code that already ships.
- **Solo build scope:** MVP reuses ~90% of the shipping "Collapse" engine: the 500-level
  procedural maze/obby layout generator, the DataStore checkpoint-save system, and the
  single-server architecture. New solo-buildable additions, in priority order: (1) horror
  lighting pass — swap default lighting for a dark/flicker ColorCorrection + Lighting rig with
  dynamic torches/emergency lights, reusable across all 500 levels with one script; (2) a single
  chaser NPC with simple pathfinding/timer-based pursuit logic that spawns behind the player and
  speeds up based on time-since-last-checkpoint; (3) a jumpscare trigger system — Touch/Region3
  events at set level intervals firing a sound + camera-shake + UI flash, using a handful of
  reusable jumpscare assets; (4) asylum-themed texture/decal reskin of existing obby geometry (no
  new geometry required); (5) ambient horror audio loop and stinger SFX. Everything else —
  checkpoint saves, leaderboards, level progression, server architecture — ships as-is from the
  existing engine, keeping this a 1-2 week solo reskin rather than a rebuild.
- **Genre tag:** Horror → Obby/Parkour (Horror-Obby hybrid).
- **Thumbnail:** first-person/over-shoulder shot of a Roblox avatar mid-jump between two
  crumbling asylum platforms, motion-blurred, with a shadowy clawed silhouette (the Chaser)
  lunging just behind them, lit by a single flickering red emergency light against near-total
  darkness; bold white/blood-red text overlay reads "500 LEVELS" in the top corner and "DON'T
  LOOK BACK" across the bottom.

### 3. The Same Door: Daily Seed Dungeon Speedrun  ·  score 7.8

- **Hook:** Every player on Earth gets the exact same procedural dungeon for 24 hours — it's
  Wordle, but you're speedrunning a maze with a stopwatch and a global leaderboard.
- **Core loop:** Player hits "Play Today's Door" → server seeds the maze/room generator with the
  day's shared daily seed (same seed for all players, all servers, resets at 00:00 UTC). Player
  spawns at the entrance; a visible timer starts. Player navigates procedurally generated
  corridors/rooms (reusing the maze-gen engine as room+corridor layout), dodging simple
  environmental hazards/locked doors/key-and-lock puzzles, no combat AI needed. Player reaches the
  exit door ("The Same Door") → timer stops → run time + death/backtrack count written to
  DataStore under today's seed key. Global + friends leaderboard shows instantly (OrderedDataStore,
  single-server-friendly, no live PvP needed). Player can retry same seed unlimited times
  pre-submission-lock to learn the optimal route, then comes back in 24h for a brand new seed.
  Optional light meta: cosmetic unlocks/streak counter for consecutive daily completions.
- **Why it trends:** It's purpose-built for the exact mechanic the buildability scout flagged as
  unclaimed: a short-session, single-server, daily-seed procedural challenge with a DataStore
  leaderboard. That structure directly targets Roblox's 2025 recommendation-algorithm shift
  toward rewarding daily return visits, the same mechanism that made Wordle-style dailies viral on
  other platforms — shareable, low-commitment, and naturally creates daily UGC ("I beat today's
  Door in 47s, can you?"). Differentiation is scored highest of the whole opportunity set (9/10)
  because research found no existing Roblox title owns this "same dungeon for everyone, resets
  daily, global speedrun board" hook — a genuine blue-ocean niche. Demand is conservative since
  it's unvalidated by CCU data, but the format rides three proven demand signals simultaneously:
  daily-challenge search behavior, dungeon-crawler/roguelike search volume, and the
  speedrun/leaderboard competitive-flex loop that drives screen-recording and sharing.
- **Solo build scope:** MVP for one dev, all systems already proven low-complexity (pure state
  machine + procgen + DataStore, zero PvP/AI/voice/audio complexity): (1) Daily seed system — a
  server script derives one integer seed per UTC calendar day (hash of date string), stored/read
  via a single DataStore key so all servers agree. (2) Maze/room generator reused as-is — feed the
  daily seed into the existing procedural maze-gen engine, output as a corridor+room dungeon
  layout, instantiated with a small kit of modular room/corridor prefabs (5-10 pieces: straight,
  corner, T-junction, dead-end, locked-door room, exit room). (3) Timer + run tracking — start on
  spawn, stop on touching exit part, store elapsed time client-predicted/server-verified. (4)
  Leaderboard — OrderedDataStore keyed by today's seed, top-N global board + a "your best today"
  stat, refreshed on run completion; reset/rotate key at UTC midnight. (5) Minimal UI — timer HUD,
  leaderboard frame, "new seed in HH:MM:SS" countdown. (6) One or two simple traversal hazards
  (locked door needing a key placed elsewhere in the maze, a simple moving-wall or timed-gate)
  generated procedurally alongside the layout. Explicitly cut from MVP: combat, NPCs/AI,
  monetization beyond passes/cosmetics, voice chat, multiplayer-in-same-run.
- **Genre tag:** Adventure → Dungeon Crawler / Roguelike (secondary: Obby-adjacent
  Speedrun/Puzzle).
- **Thumbnail:** a single glowing stone doorway centered in a dark procedurally-tiled dungeon
  corridor, viewed head-on so it reads instantly at thumbnail size. Overlay a bold stopwatch/timer
  graphic in one corner showing a dramatic time like "0:47", and large punchy text across the top
  or bottom reading "SAME SEED. EVERYONE." or "TODAY'S DUNGEON." in a bold outlined font. Optional:
  a small leaderboard-trophy icon in the opposite corner to visually signal "competitive/ranked"
  at a glance.

### 4. Outpost Zero: Sky City Tycoon  ·  score 7.05

- **Hook:** Drop your first outpost on an empty frontier sky-shelf and watch a whole procedurally
  generated city sprawl outward as your population and cash grow — no two colonies ever look the
  same.
- **Core loop:** Place your starter Outpost Pad → collect idle income from workers/droppers →
  spend cash to upgrade buildings and unlock the next procedurally-generated city block (new
  district shape/theme each time) → hit population milestones to unlock rarer block types
  (sky-rail, market district, reactor core) → prestige ("Relaunch Outpost") to reset for a
  permanent income multiplier and a fresh, differently-generated city layout → repeat, with
  leaderboard rank and cosmetic skyline pieces as the flex/status reward.
- **Why it trends:** The genre-trend report shows Town & City Sim at +65% week-over-week growth
  against just 34% saturation — the single highest opportunity score (71.6/100) this week — and
  the buildability scout independently confirms tycoon is the highest-intent search subgenre on
  Roblox. Outpost Zero rides that demand directly (title and description are built around the
  exact high-traffic search terms: "city tycoon," "town building game," "city simulator," "idle
  tycoon") while the original frontier/sky-colony theme plus procedural block generation gives it
  a differentiation edge in a field that's still mostly generic reskinned city tycoons.
- **Solo build scope:** MVP a single dev ships in ~4-6 weeks by leaning on solo-friendly Roblox
  strengths: (1) One core script system — a plot/dropper income loop (buy conveyor + workers,
  cash accrues even semi-idle) reused across every block, so no bespoke minigame code needed. (2)
  A lightweight procedural block generator: a fixed library of ~15-20 hand-built prefab "block"
  pieces (residential, market, rail, tower) placed via a simple grid/seeded-random algorithm at
  population thresholds — gives the "procedural city" hook without real procgen complexity. (3)
  DataStore (with OrderedDataStore for a Top Tycoons leaderboard) for save/load of cash, owned
  blocks, and prestige level — single-server, no cross-server sync needed for launch. (4) A basic
  upgrade shop UI (ProximityPrompt-driven), one Relaunch/prestige button, and 2-3 starter
  gamepasses (2x Cash, Auto-Collect, VIP Outpost) for monetization on day one. (5) Cut for v1:
  multiplayer trading, PvP, seasonal events — add post-launch once retention data justifies the
  extra build time.
- **Genre tag:** Simulation → Tycoon (subgenre: City/Town Building Tycoon).
- **Thumbnail:** a dramatic low-angle shot of a glowing frontier/sky-colony skyline at golden-hour,
  with one new procedurally-generated city block visibly "popping up" mid-air with sparkle VFX and
  a giant glowing cash-coin icon beside it; bold chunky text overlay "BUILD YOUR CITY!" with a
  small character pointing up in awe at the growing skyline.
