## What players want right now (convergent across all 6 scouts)

1. **Steal-and-collect/steal-and-defend is still the single dominant demand signal** —
   Steal a Brainrot set an all-time Roblox CCU record (24–25M+, beating Grow a Garden) and
   Steal An Egg rocketed to #1 on live charts within ~6–7 weeks of an Aug 2026 launch. But
   nearly every top entry recycles the same 2–3 saturated themes (brainrot, eggs, pets),
   and Reddit documents real backlash against slot-machine "Wheel" monetization bolted onto
   these games ("makes us all look bad"). **Verdict: keep the steal-and-idle-income loop,
   drop the meme skin, ship an unclaimed theme with fair (non-gambling) monetization.**
2. **Idle-grow/collector sims are still nowhere near saturated** — Grow a Garden hit 21–22M
   CCU and 1B visits in 33 days (fastest growth in Roblox history), its sequel hit 400K CCU
   within 20 minutes of launch, and Roblox itself greenlit a new farming sim ("Caramel")
   this quarter just to catch overflow demand. Clones in fresh themes still pull 8-figure
   revenue.
3. **Incremental-obby hybrids prove a tiny team can out-perform AAA Roblox studios** — +1
   Speed Keyboard Escape (2.55–5.7B visits, 6.2–6.35M peak CCU) came from a small
   independent studio, and clones still occupy 4 of the top 10 trending slots. The format
   is an "under-saturated niche led by essentially one studio."
4. **Co-op/solo horror is surging and platform-endorsed** — Roblox's own Sept 2026 Fall
   lineup names horror as a lead genre (2 of 6 official titles, including an ex-Marvel
   designer's Monster in the Mansion), and 99 Nights in the Forest sustained 220–300K CCU
   for 8+ months — real retention, not a launch spike. Every top horror title (Doors,
   Apeirophobia, Halloween: The Game) is a **fixed, hand-authored map** — nobody owns "the
   map is different every time you play."
5. **Procedural variation is the near-universally unbuilt differentiator** — regenerated
   obby courses, procedural garden/grotto plots, procedurally generated horror mazes. Every
   scout independently flags it as rare-to-absent in the current top titles, and it maps
   ~1:1 onto this dev's existing procedural-gen + DataStore + single-server toolkit.

**Avoid solo:** live real-time PvP/raiding netcode (use async "snapshot" raids instead),
AI-heavy systems, audio-heavy production (lean on lighting/VFX for horror instead of
spatial voice/sound design), anime-IP gacha reskins (licensing a solo dev can't access).

## Concept briefs (top 4)

### 1. FACILITY: Endless Nightmare 🔦👻  ·  score 7.9

- **Hook:** Every death makes you deadlier — a permadeath horror roguelite where the
  facility rebuilds itself from scratch every single run.
- **Core loop:** Drop solo or with a small crew into a freshly procedurally generated
  haunted facility (seeded room/corridor generator, reused from the maze-gen stack) →
  explore modular rooms by flashlight, collect keycards/fuses to power the exit while one
  stalking-AI monster patrols/hunts via pathfinding + line-of-sight → a run lasts 5–10
  minutes: reach the exit and extract with your Essence, or die and permadeath ends the run
  instantly → Essence banked to DataStore even on death → spend banked Essence in the hub on
  PERMANENT starting perks (extra battery, faster sprint, bonus revive, shorter cooldowns,
  starting keycard) → queue into a new seed/layout and go again.
- **Why it trends:** Roblox's own Fall 2026 lineup names co-op horror as a lead genre
  (Monster in the Mansion, from an ex-Marvel designer), and 99 Nights in the Forest held
  220–300K CCU for 8+ months — retention, not a launch spike. Every current chart-topper
  (Doors, Apeirophobia, Halloween: The Game) is a fixed, hand-authored map — nobody owns
  "the map is different every time you play," and that gap is a near-direct reuse of this
  dev's maze-gen + DataStore stack, so it ships fast without new tech risk. Leaning on
  lighting/fog for dread instead of spatial voice chat or heavy sound design sidesteps the
  audio-heavy build penalty while still delivering real horror atmosphere.
- **Solo build scope:** Reuse/adapt the existing maze-gen module to stitch ~15–20 modular
  room/corridor prefabs (one "abandoned lab/facility" kit) into a seeded layout per run,
  server-side, single-server-instance. One monster: a single stalking AI on
  PathfindingService with a 3-state machine (patrol, investigate, chase) and simple
  line-of-sight/noise triggers — no multi-monster complexity for v1. One objective type:
  collect N keycards/fuses to power the exit, with a soft run timer. Permadeath +
  extraction ends the run and returns to a small hub lobby. DataStore (ProfileService-style)
  banks Essence per run (partial credit on death, full on extraction), spent on a flat perk
  tree of ~8–10 permanent starting perks. Solo-first, co-op-ready in the same server
  instance; skip voice chat and spatial audio entirely, lean on lighting/fog/flicker. Cut
  for v1: cosmetics shop, multiple monster types, branching objectives, leaderboards beyond
  Essence total.
- **Genre tag:** Horror — Survival Horror / Escape (Roblox primary genre: Horror; best-fit
  subgenre: Survival, with Escape and Adventure as secondary discovery tags).
- **Thumbnail:** first-person view down a dim, fog-filled facility corridor lit only by a
  handheld flashlight cone and one flickering red emergency light; a tall shadowy monster
  silhouette looms at the far end at the edge of the light. Bold white/red distressed-font
  "ESCAPE OR DIE" overlay, small scratch/damage vignette around the frame edges — high
  contrast, dark background so it pops in the thumbnail grid.

### 2. Grow a Grotto 🍄💎  ·  score 7.85

- **Hook:** The "Grow a Garden" loop goes underground — plant, mine, and mutate glowing
  crystals and mushrooms in your own procedurally generated cave.
- **Core loop:** Plant spores/crystal shards on your personal cave plot → tend them
  (water/mine ticks over time, idle-friendly) → harvest fully grown crystals & mushrooms →
  sell at the Grotto Market for Shards → spend Shards on new seed/spore types, plot
  expansions, and growth-speed or luck upgrades → random mutation rolls (Glowing, Giant,
  Rainbow, Frozen, Petrified) turn common harvests into 10x–100x rare items → show off/visit
  friends' uniquely generated grottos, climb the richest-grotto leaderboard → repeat with
  bigger plots and rarer drops pulling players back for "one more grow."
- **Why it trends:** Directly rides the proven idle-grow demand curve: Grow a Garden hit
  21.3M CCU and 1B visits in 33 days (fastest growth in Roblox history), its sequel hit 400K
  CCU within 20 minutes of launch, and clones still independently pull 8-figure revenue
  (Grow a Garden 2 ~$13.2M) — the appetite isn't close to saturated. Roblox itself greenlit
  a new farming sim ("Caramel") this quarter just to catch overflow demand for this exact
  loop. Grow a Grotto claims an unclaimed sub-theme (underground/crystal/mushroom instead of
  another plant reskin) so it reads fresh in search and thumbnails, and needs only
  straightforward server validation — no combat, PvP, or AI. Procedural per-player plot
  layout is a genuine differentiator that "few clones bother to add," and is a direct reuse
  of this dev's existing procedural-gen engine.
- **Solo build scope:** Procedural per-player cave plot generator (reuse existing engine):
  rock formations, water pools, crystal veins, 3–5 plot "biome" variants for visual variety
  with zero manual level design. Core plant→grow→harvest→sell loop: 15–20 launch
  spore/crystal types, simple timer-based growth (works idle/offline), one NPC vendor UI.
  Mutation system: weighted RNG hook with 4–5 tiers (Glowing, Giant, Rainbow, Frozen,
  Petrified) multiplying sell value — the retention/virality engine, gets the most polish.
  DataStore-backed save (inventory, Shards, plot state, upgrades) with server-authoritative
  validation on every sell. Shop/upgrade UI via ProximityPrompt + ScreenGui. Single shared
  server, players can walk to other players' grottos (read-only, no trading v1). Basic
  OrderedDataStore leaderboard. Cut from v1: player-to-player trading, pets/companions,
  seasonal events, custom music/VO.
- **Genre tag:** Simulator → Idle/Grow-Sell Simulator (Collector subgenre) — same shelf as
  Farming Sim / Pet Sim, filed under Roblox's Simulator genre with idle-grow and
  mutation-collector community tags.
- **Thumbnail:** dark bioluminescent cave glowing purple/teal, centered on a giant
  Rainbow-mutated crystal mushroom dripping glowing spores, small avatar mid-harvest with a
  sparkle/mining VFX burst, bold chunky "GROW A GROTTO" title with a small "✨ RARE
  MUTATION" callout badge — mirrors the proven Grow a Garden thumbnail formula in an
  unclaimed underground palette.

### 3. Steal a Cryptid! 👻🦶  ·  score 7.55

- **Hook:** Build a lair of urban-legend monsters that print money while you sleep — then
  sneak into rival hunters' bases to steal theirs before their traps catch you.
- **Core loop:** CAPTURE — spend Cryptid Coins on Sighting Crates to hatch cryptids
  (Mothman, Bigfoot, Nessie, Chupacabra, Jersey Devil, Skinwalker, Dogman) across
  Common→Rare→Legendary→Mythic tiers → BUILD — cage cryptids in your Lair, each passively
  generating "Fear Essence" via offline-time math → DEFEND — spend earnings on traps, alarms
  and cameras that auto-trigger against intruders (scripted AI, not live PvP) → RAID —
  teleport into a private server-generated "ghost" instance replaying a snapshot of another
  player's saved base+defenses (pulled from DataStore), hold a ProximityPrompt to fill a
  steal timer while dodging the snapshot's automated defenses → PRESTIGE — rebirth to unlock
  new biomes (Forest, Swamp, Arctic Tundra, Urban Legend zone) with higher earning
  multipliers, then repeat.
- **Why it trends:** Rides the single strongest live demand signal on the platform right
  now — Steal a Brainrot posted an all-time CCU record and Steal An Egg rocketed to #1
  within 6–7 weeks — proving "collect idle income, then steal it from other players" is the
  dominant loop players are searching for and playing today. Nearly every top entry
  reskins the same 2–3 themes (brainrot, eggs, pets); cryptids/urban legends have zero
  major incumbent despite huge organic TikTok/YouTube cryptid interest, and land perfectly
  for the Sept–Oct 2026 Halloween/spooky-season search spike. Reddit documents real backlash
  against slot-machine "Wheel" monetization on these steal-tycoons — this concept's
  explicit no-gambling, transparent Game Pass/Dev Product monetization is a direct wedge
  against that resentment.
- **Solo build scope:** Base building via a single reusable plot template (grid-based
  cage/trap placement) instanced per player with CollectionService — parameterized
  template + player-chosen slots, not true proc-gen, keeping art/scripting minimal. Idle
  income: DataStore stores each cryptid's base rate + last-online timestamp; offline
  earnings computed as `(os.time() - lastSave) * rate` on join, no live replication loop.
  12–15 cryptids at launch as recolored/rescaled variants of 3–4 base meshes with different
  materials/particle auras per rarity tier. The steal layer never connects two live players:
  a raid target's state is serialized to DataStore, the attacker is teleported into a
  private server populated with scripted AI defenses (patrol/alarm/turret) — fully testable
  solo, zero netcode risk. Monetization: Game Passes + Dev Products only, explicitly no
  loot-box/wheel mechanic (also the marketing differentiator). Post-launch content as
  data-driven ModuleScript tables.
- **Genre tag:** Roblox catalog genre: Adventure (discovery surfaces this as a
  Simulator/Tycoon hybrid — tag Simulator primary, Tycoon secondary; community subgenre:
  "Steal & Defend").
- **Thumbnail:** split-composition night scene. Left: player sprinting from a rival's
  alarm-lit Lair clutching a glowing cryptid cage (shadowy Mothman silhouette, red eyes),
  red warning lights, motion-blur trail. Right: same player triumphant in their own Lair,
  rows of glowing cages (Bigfoot, Nessie, Chupacabra) behind them, Fear Essence icons
  floating up. Bold "STEAL A CRYPTID" title in cracked/glowing neon-green font, small "🎃
  HALLOWEEN UPDATE" badge in a corner for seasonal search traffic.

### 4. Factory Rush: +1 Speed Escape 🏭⚡  ·  score 7.45

- **Hook:** Every run drops you into a freshly generated conveyor-belt gauntlet — stack +1
  speed boosts, outrun the line, and bank a multiplier that never resets the same way
  twice.
- **Core loop:** Spawn on a moving assembly-line course stitched from randomized modular
  segments (conveyors, crushers, gaps, speed pads) → touching a "+1" pickup permanently
  multiplies WalkSpeed for the run → missing a jump or getting caught by the line's hazards
  ends it → reaching the end banks the run's final multiplier to a DataStore-backed "Best
  Multiplier" leaderboard → drop into a brand-new procedurally assembled course for the
  next attempt, no course ever repeats → periodic rebirth/prestige resets progress for a
  permanent multiplier bonus and access to rarer, harder factory zones.
- **Why it trends:** The +1-speed obby format already proved massive pull (2.55B–5.7B
  visits, 6.2–6.35M peak CCU) from essentially one studio, and clones still hold 4 of the
  top 10 trending slots — the demand and search behavior ("+1 speed roblox," "speed obby
  roblox," "keyboard escape alternative") are proven and current. But the format is stuck
  on hand-built static courses, capping replay value once players memorize the layout.
  Procedural regeneration is the one differentiator flagged as unaddressed in this niche —
  it directly extends session length and repeat visits without requiring combat, PvP, or
  audio systems the genre doesn't need.
- **Solo build scope:** A modular course-piece library in ReplicatedStorage (conveyors,
  gaps, crushers, speed-pad rooms) assembled at run start via a weighted-random stitcher
  script — reuses the procedural-gen background directly. A single +1 speed pickup script
  multiplying WalkSpeed and updating a run-local multiplier value. Simple hazard scripts
  (moving crushers, belt-reset kill zones) using Touched events, no custom physics.
  DataStore integration for one field (best multiplier) plus an OrderedDataStore
  leaderboard in the lobby. Minimal UI: live multiplier counter, run timer, leaderboard
  frame. Single-server, no teams/matchmaking needed since courses are per-player instanced
  or run on a shared timer loop. 1–2 gamepasses (auto-collect boosts, 2x multiplier). Free-
  catalog low-poly factory/industrial assets and stock SFX to avoid audio/art production
  burden. Ships as a tight vertical slice with 4–5 course-piece themes, expands post-launch
  by adding pieces to the same generator.
- **Genre tag:** Obby & Platformer — Speed Run / Incremental subgenre.
- **Thumbnail:** stylized low-poly factory conveyor belt racing toward the camera in forced
  perspective, small avatar mid-air blurred with speed lines, a glowing "+1" pickup
  exploding into bold neon "x847" multiplier text in the corner, bright industrial
  yellow/black hazard-stripe border to signal the obby/speed genre at a glance.
