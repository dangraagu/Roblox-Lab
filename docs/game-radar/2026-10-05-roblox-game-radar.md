## What players want right now (convergent across all 6 scouts)

1. **The steal/collect/rebirth loop is still the single hottest mechanic by raw
   concurrency** — Steal An Egg is the #1 most-played game on all of Roblox (1.5M+
   CCU), and Steal a Brainrot was the first Roblox game ever past 25M concurrent
   players. **But** it's saturated (3 of the current top-20 titles are egg/pet/steal
   sims) *and* increasingly litigated — the owner of Steal a Brainrot is actively
   pursuing legal action against IP-copying knockoffs. **Verdict: clone the loop, not
   the IP skin.**
2. **Horror + procedural escape is the strongest underserved lane for this dev's
   stack** — independently named by 4 of 6 scouts. Escape is the #2 genre on Roblox
   by CCU (763K); horror is the platform's fastest-growing genre (+40% titles since
   2023); and nearly every current horror/liminal-space leader (Doors, Piggy, Monster
   in the Mansion, Unseen Liminality — this week's single biggest weekly mover at
   +184.5% WoW) is **hand-built, not procedural** — a near 1:1 extension of the
   existing 500-level maze engine plus DataStore depth-tracking, with no PvP netcode.
3. **Tower defense and puzzle/strategy are steadier, less meme-dependent lanes**,
   specifically underexploited on DataStore-backed *persistent* progression instead of
   gacha-unit rerolls (Alliance TD, FINAL ACT TD both thriving simultaneously rather
   than one winner-take-all hit) — this rewards systems design over trend-chasing, and
   puzzle/escape logic games are flagged by two scouts as "nearly untapped."
4. **The "+1 speed keyboard/controller escape" micro-obby format is charting today**
   and is almost entirely solo-built (multiple near-identical titles sit in the live
   top 600–2,000 global ranks, one pulling ~307K CCU) — the fastest, lowest-risk thing
   this dev could ship, but with a low differentiation ceiling (near-zero original
   content, reskins of one timer+speed-boost mechanic).
5. **Co-op survival has massive durable demand but is the most production-heavy top
   genre** — 99 Nights in the Forest hit up to 14.2M peak CCU, ~26B visits, and a
   confirmed film deal — but the research repeatedly suggests a scoped-down MVP
   (procedural camp layout + simple day-counter DataStore progression) rather than a
   full content-rich clone.

Cross-cutting edge: **procedural regeneration is rare across every current top
horror, puzzle, and TD title** — most are hand-built and static — so pairing this
dev's existing proc-gen + DataStore skills with any of these genres is the genuine
differentiator. "`<game>` codes" remains a major, underused SEO lever across genres.

**Avoid solo:** 1:1 brainrot/steal IP clones (saturated + litigated + removal risk),
live-PvP raiding mechanics needing real-time netcode, gacha-unit-pull tower defense,
full-content co-op survival builds, competitive FPS, anime fighters.

## Concept briefs (top 4)

### 1. DEPTHS: Endless Backrooms Escape  ·  score 8.6

- **Hook:** Run, hide, and escape an endless liminal maze that rebuilds itself every
  time you die — the only thing that carries over is how deep you got.
- **Core loop:** Spawn into a procedurally-generated liminal hallway complex (reskinned
  from the existing maze engine) → explore rooms for keys/switches while a sound/
  light-based chaser hunts you (stealth and routing over combat) → each floor cleared
  raises depth and difficulty (more chasers, scarcer light, trickier keys) → die or get
  caught → ejected to a lobby showing "This run: depth X / Best: depth Y" (DataStore) →
  one-tap "Run Again" into a freshly reshuffled maze → optional risk/reward side-rooms
  feed a meta-progression (skins, flashlight upgrades) for returning players.
- **Why it trends:** Stacks three confirmed signals: escape is the #2 genre on Roblox
  by CCU (763K); horror is the fastest-growing genre (+40% titles since 2023) with
  "relatively few quality entries despite proven demand"; and this week's single
  biggest weekly mover was a liminal/backrooms horror title (+184.5% WoW) in a field
  where almost every current leader uses hand-built static levels. First mover to pair
  the white-hot liminal/backrooms aesthetic with true procedural regeneration.
- **Solo build scope:** Reuse the existing 500-level procedural maze engine as the
  room/hallway generator (swap tileset to liminal/backrooms: flickering fluorescents,
  carpet textures, repeating office/hotel motifs). Single-server, no PvP, no
  matchmaking, no voice, stock R15 animations. One chaser archetype with basic Roblox
  pathfinding + line-of-sight/noise detection. Minimal UI: depth counter, lobby screen,
  "Best Depth" stat. DataStoreService for per-player best depth + OrderedDataStore
  global leaderboard. Horror lift comes from audio/lighting (free/marketplace jumpscare
  SFX, dynamic flicker), not expensive VFX. Ships as a vertical slice in days.
- **Genre tag:** Horror → Escape (Procedural Survival Horror / Roguelite).
- **Thumbnail:** dark, yellow-fluorescent liminal hallway (Backrooms-style wallpaper/
  carpet) stretching into vanishing-point darkness; player sprinting toward camera with
  a flashlight beam; a barely-visible humanoid silhouette looming at the far end; bold

### 2. Steal a Relic: Vault Runners  ·  score 7.55

- **Hook:** Parkour into rival vaults, snatch glowing mythic relics, and sprint for
  your life before rebirthing into the ultimate vault boss.
- **Core loop:** Parkour through a rival's procedurally-assembled vault (wall-runs,
  dashes, trap jumps) to reach a glowing relic → grabbing it trips an alarm/guard AI,
  starting a flee timer back to your own vault past traps and patrol bots → deposit
  stolen relics to earn currency, grow your vault's size/defenses, and unlock movement
  upgrades (speed, jump, dash, grapple) + cosmetics → spend currency on rarer, harder
  vault runs → REBIRTH to cash in for a permanent multiplier, prestige skin, and access
  to higher-value vault tiers, then restart the loop at a faster pace.
- **Why it trends:** Clones the single hottest proven loop on Roblox (Steal An Egg is
  #1 most-played at 1.5M+ CCU; "Break and Steal an Egg" posted +549.6% 7-day growth)
  while sidestepping the IP risk — the owner of Steal a Brainrot is pursuing legal
  action against IP-copying knockoffs, so an original relic theme keeps the mechanic
  that works without the takedown risk. Almost no top steal-and-flee sim pairs real
  parkour/obby movement skill with the economy loop — the exact gap a solo
  obby-builder is positioned to fill.
- **Solo build scope:** One hub world + per-player/party instanced "vault" rooms
  assembled from a modular parkour-piece library via a procedural layout script.
  Server-authoritative theft (proximity/touch validated server-side, not client
  trusted); guards are patrolling NPCs with raycast line-of-sight, not networked
  opponents — no real PvP netcode. 3–5 relic rarity tiers with weighted random spawns.
  DataStore-backed profile (currency, inventory, rebirth level, upgrades) with
  session-locking to avoid dupes. Rebirth button resets for a stored permanent
  multiplier + cosmetic unlock. Simple shop UI for movement upgrades. OrderedDataStore
  leaderboard (top relics stolen / rebirths). Defer live PvP raiding, trading, and
  clan/voice systems post-launch.
- **Genre tag:** Adventure (Simulator / Obby hybrid) — "Steal-and-Flee Collector Sim"
  with a parkour/obby tag.
- **Thumbnail:** low-angle shot of a character mid wall-run, leaping over a red laser
  tripwire grid toward a glowing purple-and-gold relic on a pedestal in a dark stone
  vault; alarm lights flashing red; motion-blur speed streaks; bold "STEAL IT!" text
  with a small relic-and-coin icon.

### 3. LockShift: Puzzle Escape Gauntlet  ·  score 7.15

- **Hook:** Every lock, every key, every room — reshuffled on every run. Think fast,
  solve smart, and beat the clock before the gauntlet beats you.
- **Core loop:** Drop into a gauntlet of 6–10 procedurally assembled rooms. Each room
  randomly combines a lock type (color-match, symbol sequence, number dial,
  switch/logic-gate) with a key mechanic scattered somewhere in the room — forcing a
  fresh puzzle approach every run, even on a familiar room "shape." Solve the room's
  logic to unlock its door and advance while a live timer runs. Reaching the final
  vault stops the clock, saves completion time to DataStore, and posts it to a global
  leaderboard — driving a tight "one more run" loop with zero combat or grinding.
- **Why it trends:** Two scouts independently flag Puzzle/Escape as low-competition
  with stable demand — "nearly untapped" and "underserved despite consistent demand" —
  unlike saturated obby/simulator/horror categories. Procedural recombination answers
  the top complaint about escape/puzzle games (solved once, never replayed), and a
  global speedrun leaderboard taps the same competitive-sharing behavior that drives
  obby/speedrun virality without needing combat or an economy.
- **Solo build scope:** Reuse existing procedural-generation code to assemble a
  gauntlet from ~15–20 modular room templates, each pre-built with 2–4 randomizable
  lock-key logic variants, so the generator just permutes room order + lock variant +
  key placement per run. Single-server authoritative Lua validates each lock solve (no
  client trust, no anti-cheat complexity). Minimal art: grayscale/greebled block-kit
  rooms differentiated by accent color per lock type, default avatars, no custom
  rigging. Simple HUD (room counter + timer) and end screen with completion time,
  personal best, and leaderboard rank. DataStore for personal-best time + OrderedData
  Store top-100 board on a lobby leaderboard part. Stretch goals post-launch: cosmetic
  win-trail gamepass, reroll/skip gamepass, more room template packs.
- **Genre tag:** Puzzle → Escape Room / Logic Puzzle (primary Roblox discovery tag:
  Puzzle).
- **Thumbnail:** split composition — left, a glowing padlock cracking open with
  circuit-like light lines; right, a stopwatch mid-countdown with motion-blur speed
  lines; bold neon title text across the middle, high-contrast red/blue lighting, a
  default-avatar silhouette reaching toward a glowing vault door in the background.

### 4. Tower Legacy TD  ·  score 7.0

- **Hook:** The only tower defense where your towers never reset — unlock once,
  defend forever, across every map and run.
- **Core loop:** Drop into a map, place unlocked towers along the lane, and hold off
  escalating waves of original-lore enemies (grunts → armored → flyers → bosses).
  Waves pay out Essence (soft currency) and Cores (rare currency). Spend Essence to
  permanently level a tower's stats and Cores to permanently unlock new towers/
  abilities — all written to a single account-wide DataStore profile. Finish a map (or
  push an Endless mode for bonus multipliers) and jump into a different map with the
  same roster and levels already unlocked, no gacha reroll. Weekly events and
  redeemable codes inject bursts of Cores/Essence to pull players back.
- **Why it trends:** Alliance TD (5.1M visits, 93.7% rating) and FINAL ACT TD (10.2M
  visits, 78.9% rating) prove Roblox TD has durable, loop-driven demand with room for
  multiple mid-size winners rather than one meme-chasing hit — but both, and the rest
  of the field, run on gacha unit pulls that force players to re-roll progress every
  match, the exact friction point behind searches like "roblox td progression." Tower
  Legacy TD answers that search intent literally with a DataStore-backed, account-wide
  save, while an original-lore theme sidesteps the crowded anime/brainrot-skin lane.
- **Solo build scope:** MVP ships with 4 original towers (Archer, Cannon, Frost Mage,
  Support Totem), each with 3 permanent upgrade tiers; 2 maps built from one reusable,
  data-driven wave/pathing module (new maps = new waypoint + enemy-table assets, not
  new code); 3 enemy types (basic, armored, flying) on a shared modular enemy script;
  one Endless mode reusing the same wave generator; a single DataStore-backed
  PlayerProfile (unlocked towers, upgrade levels, currencies) read by every map, no
  per-map save silos; an OrderedDataStore leaderboard for best Endless wave; a
  lightweight codes-redemption system for live-ops without new code pushes. Cut from
  MVP: PvP, trading, voice, multiplayer co-op lobbies, procedural map generation beyond
  hand-built waypoints.
- **Genre tag:** Tower Defense / Strategy & Defense (Meta-Progression TD).
- **Thumbnail:** a single glowing tower on a dark battlefield lane, half its silhouette
  made of swirling blue "save file" data particles rising into a translucent HUD panel
  showing tower icons with permanent checkmarks; bold condensed "TOWER LEGACY TD" top
  third; a small "NO RESETS" corner-stamped badge; waves of enemies silhouetted
  charging from the right in warm orange light against the cool blue tower glow.
