# Lost & Found Depot in real Studio

## Eye-candy, first look (night shift, 2026-10-09)

The place was built from `src/` and played solo for 16 s, with quality pinned to Level21. The screenshot is
`marketing/studio-2026-10-09/01-spawn.jpg` (1920 x 795, the Studio viewport). The Studio store gate logged
"the live stores are OFF", as intended. A scan found no visible HUD frame without text.

- The console shows the gate line, the store lines, "loaded: 12 bays" and the shift seed. There are no errors.
- **Cluttered labels.** The item tags over the sorting tables ("E·4·YELLOW Wallet", "T·2·GREEN Teddy Bear" ...)
  overlap each other and the wing signs ("BAGS / WEA...", "...TRONICS") from the spawn view. A player has to walk
  close to read them.
- Roblox's chat notice prints over the CASH panel for its first seconds.
- Not measured: the sort loop (verified 2026-09-17), and the wings and weather per wing.
