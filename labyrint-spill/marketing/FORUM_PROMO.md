## Read this first: sequencing (why timing matters)

The game is still limited to **16+ and trusted friends** until age/ID verification
clears. That splits the venues into two groups:

- **Developer-feedback venues** (audience = other devs looking at your thumbnails,
  description, and design) — **fine to post now.** They don't need to actually play
  to give useful feedback, and pre-launch feedback is exactly what they're for.
- **Player-facing venues** (audience = kids who want to play right now) — **hold
  until verification clears.** If you send players to a game they can't enter, they
  bounce, and on Reddit a "my game" post about a locked game tends to get downvoted
  or removed. That first impression is hard to redo.

Post to the dev venues now; queue the player venues for launch week.

- I can't create accounts, and I won't spray posts across your logged-in accounts

## Rules of thumb (so accounts don't get flagged)

- One post per community, in the correct subforum/channel; don't repost the same link
  around.
- Frame dev venues as "feedback," not "come play" — that's what they reward.
- Never mass-DM the link. Never buy visits/likes.
- Respond to replies; engagement is what keeps a thread alive and ranking.
