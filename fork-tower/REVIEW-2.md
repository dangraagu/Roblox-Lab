## Still open (5)

### THE CORE LOOP IS DOMINATED: the optimal way to play Fork Tower is to never read an inscription. This is the headline finding of this pass and neither the builder nor the first review named it.

**Proof:** Measured over 3000 seeds x 10 floors with my own probe against the real Fork/Section/Build modules. (a) The trapped door's trait is stronger on 41.50% of forks, weaker on 41.71%, equal on 16.79% - mean advantage +0.00 stars. By Config.Fairness.PowerBalanceTolerance this is deliberate, and it means the trap is uncorrelated with reward. (b) Four strategies, 3000 seeds each: always-safe (read the inscription, dodge every trap) mean score 37.10, 70 platforms, Legendarisk 2%, Episk 22%. Always-strongest (ignore the inscription entirely; just take the door with more stars) mean score 45.26, 90 platforms, Legendarisk 39%, Episk 49%. (c) Always-strongest beats always-safe on 95.1% of seeds, ties 2.2%, loses 2.7%. (d) The star count is printed on every door in the world - src/server/Main.server.luau billboard(door, "TraitLabel", trait.label .. "\n" .. string.rep("*", trait.power)) - so the dominant signal is rendered LARGER than the inscription it dominates. (e) The trap's only cost is time: hazards return you to a checkpoint that moves up with you, so nothing is ever lost. 70 vs 90 platforms is roughly 15-25 seconds on a ~54s-of-pure-movement run. (f) The ordered leaderboard ranks on bestScore, so it actively rewards ignoring the mechanic. Net: the 40,000-fork fairness spec, the sign-exclusivity proof, the liar mechanic and WITNESS E all defend a decision the reward curve has already settled. The inscription is a tiebreaker for the 16.79% of forks where the stars are equal.

### MUTATION SURVIVED - the hazard-clearance guard is a bare non-overlap test, so the 2.00-stud number finding 3's fix rests on is defended by nothing.

**Proof:** M2: in src/shared/Section.luau I halved the respawn offset (`local inner = (p.size / 2 - cfg.World.CharacterHalfWidth) / 2`). Every suite stayed green: Fork 53/0, Section 47/0, Build 31/0, Codes 19/0, Rng 32/0, responsive 70/0, headless 85/0 - identical to baseline. My own measurement under that mutation: minimum hazard clearance drops from 2.00 studs to 0.75 studs, i.e. below the CharacterHalfWidth=2 the whole model is built on. The reason is tests/Section.spec.luau lines 346-350, which only ask `if ox > 1e-9 and oz > 1e-9 then inHazard += 1`, and the same >1e-9 test in Section.check - both are satisfied by any clearance above zero. Any future tuning of CharacterHalfWidth, HazardBite, HazardSize or TileSize can shrink the clearance to 0.001 studs with all 337 tests green.

### MUTATION SURVIVED - the finding-2 headline claim ('MaxLanes is a FLOOR, so a Studio slider can no longer put more players in the server than there are towers') is asserted by no test.

**Proof:** M5: in src/server/Main.server.luau I flipped `local laneCount = math.max(Config.World.MaxLanes, tonumber(Players.MaxPlayers) or 0)` to math.min - the exact regression that re-opens the coupling the review found missing. All six specs and the headless check stayed at baseline (85/0). Running the headless check under the mutation prints `crowd: 12 players seated, 21 unseated` instead of the baseline `24 players seated, 9 unseated`, and still passes, because check_forktower.luau line 900 only asserts `truthy(seated > 0, ...)`. The seated COUNT is never compared to the pool size.

### PRE-EXISTING, found by me, missed by both passes: a spent skip is refunded as a penalty section on rejoin. The player loses the skip AND gets the trap.

**Proof:** My world probe: player with 3 skips armed a skip and took the trapped door on floor 1 -> Section_1 built with Penalty=false, 5 platforms (the skip worked). The player then left and rejoined, exactly as closing and reopening the game -> Section_1 rebuilt with Penalty=true, 9 platforms. Cause: onDoorChosen writes p.slots[level] and decrements p.skips, but nothing records THAT the skip was spent on that floor; profile.armedSkip is explicitly runtime-only. buildLane then recomputes `local trapped = Fork.isTrap(floorData, slot)` and rebuilds the penalty section. Because Section.build mixes PENALTY_MIX into the seed, the penalty section is a completely different walk, so lane.entry[level] moves too and the world changes under the player mid-climb. Skips are the monetisable resource (Config.Passes.ExtraSkip).

## Broken BY the fixes (3)

### A player the server could not seat is NEVER handed a lane that later frees up - and the pad they are standing on tells them they are waiting for one. This is new: before the fix they got a (broken, overlapping) tower; now they get a permanent dead state.

**Proof:** My world probe: filled all 24 lanes, then had a SEATED player (Crowd1) leave and advanced 5 seconds. `after 5 s, Crowd19 has a lane: false`. claimLane() is only ever called from buildLane, which is only called from PlayerAdded and from RebirthEvent - nothing re-runs it when teardownLane frees an index, so the only escape is for the player to leave and rejoin the server themselves. Meanwhile src/server/Main.server.luau builds the waiting pad with billboard(pad, "WaitingLabel", "FORK TOWER\nVenter paa et ledig taarn ...") - 'waiting for a free tower' - a promise the server does not keep, while the toast on the same event says 'proev en annen server' (try another server). The two messages contradict each other and the sign is the one that stays on screen.

### The new respawn point is FLUSH with the platform edge: 0.00 studs of margin, down from 2.50 at the old (broken) platform centre. The fix picked the extreme end of the feasible band rather than its middle, and nothing in this repo can tell whether that is safe.

**Proof:** Measured over all 414,160 hazards: `min platform-edge margin 0.00 studs`, and confirmed in the built world - `platform box X[238.0..247.0]; character-box margin to the edge: X 0.00, Z 2.50`. The arithmetic: the clear band on the far side of the hazard is 6 studs wide, so the character centre may sit anywhere in a 2-stud interval; Section.luau chooses `inner = p.size/2 - CharacterHalfWidth` = 2.5, the far end, which buys 2.00 studs from the hazard and spends all 2.50 studs of edge margin. The midpoint (inner = 1.5) would give 1.00/1.00 instead of 2.00/0.00. Section.check's own test `math.abs(rp.dx - p.dx) + ch > p.size/2 + 1e-9` passes at exactly 4.5 > 4.5 + 1e-9 = false, i.e. the shipping value sits precisely on the guard's boundary. The player is also dropped onto that spot from RespawnLift = 4 studs up. Whether a real Humanoid stays on it is exactly the thing the emulator cannot answer.

### The new hazard debounce is per LANE, not per hazard, so one hit makes every hazard in the whole tower inert for a full second.

**Proof:** src/server/Main.server.luau keeps a single `lane.hazardReadyAt` and the hazard Touched handler gates on `if t < lane.hazardReadyAt then return end`, setting it to `t + Config.Limits.HazardCooldown` (1 s) for the lane, not for the hazard. At WalkSpeed 16-22 that second covers 16-22 studs of travel, and adjacent platforms are only 11-15.5 studs apart (reach = TileSize + gap), so a player who eats one hazard walks through the next one or two for free. Low severity - a hazard only costs one platform of progress anyway - but it is new code and it means the 'more hazards' half of the penalty section is weaker than the platform count suggests.
