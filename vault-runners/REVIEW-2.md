# Vault Runners - review after the first round of fixes

**Verdict: SHIP-WITH-DEFERRED**

Second pass, 2026-09-10. The reviewer verified each claimed fix with its OWN measurement
rather than by re-running the fixer's tests, and then went looking for what the fixing
broke. Read alongside REVIEW.md, which is the first pass.

## Independently confirmed closed (7)

- Finding 1 - the unwinnable vaults. Verified with MY OWN simulator (D:/Claude/Roblox/vault-runners, scratch file since deleted): my own BFS over the raw MazeGen grids, my own clock (2*cellSize per cell step at WalkSpeed 24, 6 treads x 0.9s per storey), and my own kill arithmetic read straight off model.killPlane.startY/endY - I did not call VaultPath.measure or RunState.caught. Swept 3 tiers x 400 floors = 1200 floors: 0 unwinnable. Tightest per-storey margin +9.9s (Silver floor 221, storey 0); tightest escape margin +30.2s (Bronze floor 6); longest countdown 258s (Gold f1) against MaxSeconds 300; shortest 63s against MinSeconds 30. CONTROL, to prove my harness can fail: fed it the OLD geometry (KillPlaneStart -10 absolute) and the OLD typed countdowns at the review's WalkSpeed 16 with free climbing - 'Silver f1 worst storey 3 slack -17.8s -> UNWINNABLE', 'Gold f1 worst storey 4 slack -64.9s -> UNWINNABLE', Bronze f1 marginal at +1.7s. Same three verdicts the original review reached.

- Finding 1b - the spec is permanent and load-bearing. tests/Collapse.spec.luau is 274 assertions and it caught three separate model mutations I applied to source: VaultPath.studsPerStep 2*cellSize -> cellSize (Collapse 272/2 + headless 105/1), the per-storey deadline loop deleted from VaultPath.countdown (Collapse 273/1, and nothing else in the tree noticed - exactly the failure mode the fixer described), and Config.Vault.KillPlaneLeadStoreys 1 -> 0 (Collapse hard-errors, VaultFloor 187/14, headless hard-errors). Config.Collapse.Slack 2.0 -> 5.0 and MinSlack 1.5 -> 0.5 were also killed, so the slack is bounded on BOTH sides.

- Finding 2 - beginRun with no character. My own probe: joined a player and never called simulateSpawn (plr.Character == nil, the real 5-second Players.RespawnTime window), fired Enter, advanced 2s. No vault was built. Then simulateSpawn and fire again: the vault IS built, so the guard is not a permanent lockout. Reverting it to the shipped shape (check moved back down, teleport re-wrapped in 'if hrp then') gives headless 103/3: 'a player with no body builds no vault -> got 1, want 0', 'and is told why rather than being silently ignored', 'the HUD was never told a run started'.

- Finding 3 - rejected remotes writing to the DataStore. My own counter off the emulator's store.__calls, five different rejection paths, each 60 fires followed by 15s of virtual time (3 full flush ticks, so a coalesced write had every chance to land): equip a pet not owned = 0 calls; buy a 12000-gem pet with 2000 gems = 0; equip a pet id that does not exist = 0; buy a pet id that does not exist = 0; equip with a NUMBER instead of a string = 0. 120 alternating REAL buys in one burst = 1 call. 120 seconds of a player standing still = 0 calls (the old 45s autosave wrote unconditionally). Both halves of the fix are load-bearing: deleting the fingerprint early-return gives headless 103/3 ('60 REFUSED equips ... wrote 1 times -> got 1, want 0' x3), and routing requestSave straight to flush gives 105/1 ('120 alternating REAL equips cost exactly one DataStore call ... -> got 119, want 1').

- Finding 4 - Timer over Wallet on portrait phones. Recomputed the mid-run top edge MYSELF from src/shared/Responsive.luau plus the literal constants in Hud.client.luau - no emulator, no hudcheck.luau - across 11 viewports including three the project does not check (390x844, 320x568, 2560x1440), in BOTH the hub and mid-run. Zero wallet/timer overlaps, zero panels off screen, and the toast clears whichever of the two ends up lowest at every size. My arithmetic reproduces the original defect exactly (414x800: wallet 14..314, timer 205..485 = 109 design px = 65 screen px at scale 0.60). Reverting the fix ('stacked = false') gives 'FAIL - 2 problem(s)' from check_vaulthud, which now measures 22 layouts (11 viewports x hub/mid-run, 360x640 included) by firing the real Run remote.

- Finding 5 - session-lock release coverage. My own probe against the stored record, not a rejoin: joining stamps a jobId and a lockUntil in the future; leaving clears BOTH (jobId == nil AND lockUntil == 0); a rejoin re-acquires immediately. The review's exact mutation ('old.jobId = releaseLock and nil or game.JobId') is now killed: headless 105/1, 'FAIL: LEAVING RELEASES THE OWNER STAMP -> got robloxemu-headless, want nil'. luau-analyze over all 14 src files reports zero MisleadingAndOr.

- Finding 6 - the part-budget overclaim. The assertion no longer claims a global maximum: it now reads 'the worst floor found anywhere in %d floors', with a sampled >= 360 guard and a second guard asserting that cap-pinned floors produce MORE THAN ONE distinct part count. README.md and CLAUDE.md no longer contain the string '618'. My own independent sweep of 6000 floors (3 tiers x 2000) finds a true worst of 326 parts at Gold floor 721 - larger than the spec's sampled 314 at Silver f42, which is the point the honest wording now concedes, and 4.6x under the 1500 budget. 5970 cap-pinned floors produce 37 distinct part counts, confirming a single sample can never be a maximum.

## Still open (4)

### Finding 8 is MITIGATED, not closed, and by a much smaller factor than the write-up implies. The report says a cheater 'still gets the straight-line route through the maze at walking pace'. It is not walking pace: Trace's ceiling is 1.35x WalkSpeed horizontally (32.4 studs/s) and 2.5x the modelled climb rate (8.33 studs/s vs the honest 3.33), and the flat and up budgets are spent CONCURRENTLY, so a climb costs max(walk, climb) instead of walk-then-climb. Combined with wall-blindness that is a 4.1x-6.0x advantage on the game's only economy.

**Proof:** My own greedy full-clear tour of every gem, honest maze route vs the best tour Trace still permits, both at 0.2s resolution. Bronze f1: honest 72.8s vs cheat 17.6s (4.1x). Silver f10: honest 258.2s vs cheat 42.8s (6.0x). Gold f42: honest 289.6s vs cheat 60.2s (4.8x). Decomposed: wall-blindness alone is 2.2x-3.8x (the documented, unavoidable half), and the TOLERANCE CONSTANTS add a FURTHER 1.5x-1.9x on top - that half is a tuning choice, not a limitation. The economic consequence is that on 7 of the 9 floors I measured the honest player CANNOT full-clear inside the countdown while the cheater can: Gold f42 honest 289.6s against a 168s countdown, cheat 60.2s. Live-server confirmation that the throttle does engage at all: booting the real server and holding the root part on the ExitPad ends Bronze f1 after 3.0s with reason=escaped and banked=0 - the teleport itself is throttled and banks nothing, which is real progress over the shipped 'instant 100%'.

### The anti-cheat's own tuning constants have NO test floor. Three mutations to Config.Run's trusted-movement block survive the entire suite - 819 unit assertions across 8 specs, 106 headless assertions, and check_vaulthud PASS - all green.

**Proof:** TrustedSpeedFactor 1.35 -> 3.0 (72 studs/s, 3x the honest walk): Trace.spec 0 failed, headless 0 failed, HUD PASS. TrustedClimbFactor 2.5 -> 10 (33.3 studs/s up, a whole storey in 0.54s): 0 failed, 0 failed, PASS. TrustedBurstSeconds 3 -> 60: 0 failed, 0 failed, PASS. I bisected the speed factor and the coverage is not even monotonic - 2.0 fails one headless assertion, 3.0 passes everything, 4.0 fails one Trace assertion. The one assertion that does fire ('a gem three storeys away is not picked up by claiming to be on it') is a knife-edge on one gem's geometry, not a bound. tests/Trace.spec.luau (23 assertions) proves the throttle EXISTS; nothing anywhere proves it is TIGHT.

### Config.Save.FlushTickSeconds = 5 is below the write cadence the fix's own comment cites. Main.server's flush() header and Config.Save both say Roblox 'throttles writes to one key to roughly one every six seconds'; the coalescing window is five.

**Proof:** My own measurement against the real server: 40 real profile changes spaced one flush tick apart cost 40 DataStore calls over 220s = one write per 5.5s. A client alternating EquipEvent between two OWNED pets is a real profile change every time, so it sustains that rate indefinitely - roughly 36 writes in three minutes against a 30-deep queue. Two orders of magnitude better than the shipped 60-writes-per-burst, and worth a one-character change rather than a block.

### Finding 7 (no obby) is not fixed, as disclosed - and it is the gate on PUBLISHING rather than on the code. The generator's only vertical challenge is still the six-step spiral in one corner cell; nothing crumbles and there is no jump a player can fail, while the brief's genre line, thumbnail concept and store copy all lead with 'ROBLOX RAGE OBBY'.

**Proof:** Confirmed the disclosure landed: README.md:138 opens the not-built list with 'THERE IS NO OBBY', README.md:8 says 'It is not one, and it never was', and CLAUDE.md:141 files it as a product decision. I did not re-verify the generator because the review's own reading of VaultFloor.luau:131-164 is unchanged in the current tree. Shipping the code is fine; shipping the STORE PAGE as written is not, and that decision is still open.

## Broken BY the fixes (2)

### The floor counter stopped being a difficulty curve. Config.Curve's caps were cut from 8 cells / 7 storeys to 6 / 5 to hold the countdown under MaxSeconds 300 - but Gold's base tier is ALREADY 6 cells x 5 storeys, so Gold floor 1 starts at both caps and never grows. From floor 26 onward every floor of every tier is the identical difficulty spec. The 6000-gem Gold unlock buys a tier whose entire difficulty curve is 25 floors of slack tightening, then flat forever. The fixer disclosed this as 'thin'; measured, it is flat.

**Proof:** My own sweep of Progression.difficulty + VaultPath.countdown. Cells max out at Bronze floor 13 / Silver floor 7 / GOLD FLOOR 1; storeys at Bronze 21 / Silver 11 / GOLD FLOOR 1; gems at 26/21/16; slack bottoms out at MinSlack for all three at floor 26. Gold floor 26 vs floor 200 vs floor 2000 are byte-identical specs: cells 6/6/6, storeys 5/5/5, gems 9/9/9, slack 1.50/1.50/1.50. Only the maze seed differs.

### The countdown now swings hard in both directions between adjacent floors, so 'deeper' regularly means 'shorter and easier'. This is the direct cost of deriving the countdown from the maze that generated, and it is what a player reads off the floor counter.

**Proof:** Measured tightness (optimal route seconds / countdown seconds) over floors 1..60: 24 of 59 consecutive Bronze floors, 23 of 59 Silver and 25 of 59 Gold are EASIER than the floor immediately before them - about 41% of the ladder runs backwards. Wall-clock: Bronze f1..f10 countdowns are 78, 65, 69, 64, 87, 63, 96, 80, 116, 93 seconds; Gold f1 is a 258s run and Gold f9 is 184s, 74 seconds shorter and nominally deeper.
