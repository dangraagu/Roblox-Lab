THE SAME DOOR - one dungeon, the same for everyone, new every day.

Every day at 00:00 UTC the dungeon rearranges itself around the same old Door. Every player on Roblox gets the exact same layout that day. Find the 3 Seals, open the Door, and your time goes on today's board.

- The clock starts when you cross the line and stops at the Door. Retry as often as you like: your best time counts.
- Learn the halls, find the fastest order, cut the corners.
- Earn Bronze, Silver, Gold, and the Sealer medal for a run within 10% of the perfect line.
- Watch the cracked ceilings: a red ring shows exactly where the rock will land.
- Public and friends boards. A tie goes to whoever got there first.
- Keep your Door streak alive, day after day.

Nothing to buy. No pay-to-win. Just you, today's Door, and everyone else trying the same one.
